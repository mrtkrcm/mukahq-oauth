mukahq privacy policy

mukahq reads Gmail messages for the account owner who authorised it, using the read-only Gmail scope. It does not send, modify or delete mail.

Data read through Google APIs stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored in the operating system's keychain. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

mukahq's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
